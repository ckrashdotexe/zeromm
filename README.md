# ZERO MM MINERALS LLP
### Premium Digital Experience & Industrial Materials Portfolio

A bespoke, production-grade digital experience for **ZERO MM MINERALS LLP**, an industrial minerals and supply enterprise associated with mining, quarrying, and mineral processing in Maharashtra, India.

> **Core Brand Idea:** "Material, in its purest form."  
> **Supporting Positioning:** "Industrial minerals. Thoughtfully sourced. Built for application."

---

## 1. Architectural Highlights & Creative Philosophy

- **Physical Materiality:** Crafted around genuine geological textures, crystalline structures, and precision-sieved particle physics instead of generic corporate templates.
- **Strict Anti-AI-Slop Standard:** Zero floating blobs, zero decorative gradients, zero fake testimonials, zero fabricated numbers, zero stock businessmen, and no repetitive rounded card grids.
- **Museum-Quality Imagery:** Bespoke high-resolution editorial photography of all 9 product families under raking directional studio illumination on graphite/slate plinths.
- **Restrained Motion Design:** Non-blocking entrance sequences, mask reveals, and tactile hover states adhering strictly to `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 2. Nine Product Families

### 01. Mineral Powders & Grains
1. **Dolomite — Powder & Grains** (`CaMg(CO3)2`) — Steelmaking & foundry fluxing, container glass, and industrial coatings.
2. **Calcite Powder** (`CaCO3`) — High-brightness functional extender for emulsion paints, rigid PVC pipes, and masterbatches.
3. **Talc & Soapstone Powder** (`Mg3Si4O10(OH)2`) — Platy lamellar structure for automotive polypropylene, barrier primers, and ceramics.
4. **Silica — Sand & Powder** (`SiO2`) — High-silica quartz sand and micro-crystalline silica flour for foundry cores and glass melting.
5. **Pyrophyllite Powder** (`Al2Si4O10(OH)2`) — Low thermal expansion and slag-resistant hydrous aluminium silicate for refractory castables.

### 02. Clays & Oxides
6. **China Clay & Kaolin Clay Powder** (`Al2Si2O5(OH)4`) — Beneficiated hydrous aluminosilicate for ceramics, paper sizing, and architectural emulsions.
7. **Iron Oxide & Red Oxide Powder** (`Fe2O3`) — Natural hematite pigment with high tinting strength for anti-corrosive primers and concrete pavers.
8. **Coloured Grains & Aggregates** — Calibrated natural mineral granules for epoxy terrazzo flooring and exterior exposed plasters.

### 03. White Marble Powder
9. **White Marble Powder** (`CaCO3`) — High-refractive metamorphic marble powder for polymer wall putties, Marmorino stuccos, and engineered stone.

## 3. Technology Stack & Design Tokens

- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 8
- **Styling:** Custom CSS Architecture & Design Token System with Tailwind CSS v4
- **Routing:** React Router v7
- **Motion:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger + CSS hardware-accelerated transforms
- **Icons:** Lucide React
